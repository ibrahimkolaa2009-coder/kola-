About us

A team built like a machine. Every part has a job.

Apexion is a student robotics team competing in the National Robotics League (NRL) 2026. We design, build, wire and program our own robot, document every step, and share the journey with our community.

01 · Innovate

Think past the obvious

We question every design choice and look for the smarter mechanism, the cleaner circuit and the faster line of code.

02 · Build

Make it real, make it work

Ideas only count once they run. We cut, assemble, wire, test and rebuild until Vortex Ion performs every time.

03 · Impact

Lift others with us

We share what we learn through workshops, content and outreach, so more students start building robots of their own.

Our mission

To design and build Vortex Ion, a robot that performs with precision under pressure, and to prove what a student team can engineer when different minds work toward one vision.

Our vision

Precision beyond limits. We want Apexion to stand for engineering that is careful, creative and fearless, on the NRL stage and long after it.
